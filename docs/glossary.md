# Glossary

- **SKU** - stock keeping unit, `CATEGORY-NUMBER-VARIANT`.
- **Cart line** - one product variant and its quantity in a cart.
- **Reservation** - stock held for an order until it ships or is cancelled.
- **Fulfilment** - picking, packing and handing parcels to the carrier.
- **Cut-off** - the latest order time for same-day dispatch.
- **Authorisation** - the issuer reserved the amount on the card.
- **Capture** - the reserved amount is actually charged.
